Authentic Indian cooking, on Park Road, for the better part of forty years.

Curry Royal has been a quiet fixture in the suburbs of Loughborough since 1988. A family-run kitchen turning out traditional Indian dishes — proper biryanis, tandoori from the clay oven, curries built up from whole spices rather than shortcuts — to be eaten at home, on the sofa, at your own table.

The room is small. The recipes are not. Pick up the phone, place a collection, or have it brought to the door — whichever suits the evening.

The Bill of Fare
